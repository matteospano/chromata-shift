How to play (?)
Reach the target color.

Brighten and Darken move one shade. Invert switches to the opposite hue and flips the shade. Mix with Red, Green or Blue repeatedly halves the remaining hue distance and moves one shade toward neutral.

Moves counts button presses. Cost adds each button's price. Path distance adds hue and shade cells crossed; inversion always counts as 6d. White and black are terminal.

Level 01 · Learn the inversion

Every color is a state.

Transform the starting color into the target. Each move follows a fixed rule; the challenge is choosing a route that reaches the goal without wasting moves, cost, or color-space.
